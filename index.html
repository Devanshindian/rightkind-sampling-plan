About people

  • Our buyers already take protein or want more of it. They need a reason to pick us.
  • RightKind costs a little more than other brands, so our buyers choose on taste and will pay for it.
  • They already buy health products online and on Blinkit, so once they like it, ordering takes minutes.
  • Tasting on the spot builds far more intent than a take-home sachet.
  • Seeing the brand twice (at work and at home, or at a run and at home) makes people remember it. That is why we cluster.

A persona makes the cut only if our pitch (taste-first, dairy-free protein that feels like an upgrade) fixes a real problem for them, they can pay a premium and buy again, and we can reach many of them cheaply in one place.

05 · The sampling moment

Screen, taste, feedback, all in a few minutes

1

Soft screen, 20 seconds

2

Taste 2 flavours side by side

3

Give feedback

4

Register on WhatsApp

5

Leave with an offer code

Soft screen

  1. Do you take any protein product now, or are you trying to add more protein to your diet?
  2. Do you work out or play a sport at least once a week, or avoid dairy?
  3. Do you order groceries or health products online?

We prefer people who say yes to 1 or 2, and yes to 3.

Taste

  • We open each sachet in front of people and shake it into 3 small cups of about 80 ml.
  • Each person tastes 2 flavours and picks one. We rotate the pairs so every flavour gets compared.

Feedback

  • How much did you like it, 1 to 5?
  • Would you buy it?
  • Which of the two did you prefer?
  • What would you change?

Captured while they drink. Every answer is saved against their registration, so it doubles as product research.

What pulls a crowd

  • Protein check: people enter their weight and meals and see the protein they need against what they eat. Doubles as screening.
  • Blind taste test against a leading protein shake, with a live scoreboard, in societies where people have time.

06 · Stall setup and team

Two people at every stall, two stalls at most

Who runs a stall

  • Brand lead (RightKind team): pitches, pours the cups, answers questions, sells
  • Brand associate (college intern hired for the campaign): screens people, points them to the QR code, hands out cups
  • Creator: films reactions for RightKind's Instagram
  • Team: two crews, so 2 people from the RightKind team and 2 interns

Stall kit, 2 sets

Branded table and backdrop, claim and QR standees, shakers, cold box, tablet. Run clubs: a folding table and the QR standee.

Selling on the spot

Society and tech park stalls sell sachets at Rs 150 and 8-sachet boxes at Rs 999. Run clubs don't sell.

Training

A 2-hour brief, a 30-second pitch and ready answers to common questions.

Personalised messages on days 0, 3, 10 and 21, based on each person's flavour, feedback, protein check result and where we met them.

What we measure
